Recommended Approach

We can continue driving usage across critical workflows to maintain operational control and visibility. At the same time, enhancements should move in parallel so the platform becomes easier to use, more insightful, and more motivating for the team. This creates a healthier balance between short-term discipline and long-term adoption.

Enforce the essentials now. Enhance the experience in parallel.
